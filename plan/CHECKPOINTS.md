# Checkpoints and Milestones

## Weekly mini-check (Sunday, 15 min)
Score yourself 0-2 on each. Log in `tracking/PROGRESS.md`.
1. **Say it cold:** pick 5 sentences from this week's grammar; say each in <= 5 seconds, no notes.
2. **Shadow test:** replay Tuesday's clip; is your recording closer to the speaker than last week?
3. **Retrieve:** list 10 words learned this week from memory (write or say them).
4. **Vault spot-check:** 5 random Vault cards (things you already knew); any you hesitated on?
5. **Streak:** did you hit at least the 5-minute floor on 6 of 7 days?

Score 8-10 = on track. 5-7 = keep going, add one extra drill. <5 = reduce load, find the blocker.

## Milestone tests (every 4-6 weeks)
Record yourself for all of these (audio or video). A tutor or fluent relative reviews them.

| # | When | Speaking task | Reading/writing task | Pass criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wk 4 | 2-min self-introduction, family, routine | Read 100-word paragraph aloud | Correct vowels/digraphs; fewer than 5 pauses > 3s |
| 2 | Wk 10 | 5-min tutor conversation (unscripted) | Write 100 words about your week | <= 10 grammar errors; explain 3 rules |
| 3 | Wk 14 | 10-min conversation on surprise topic | Summarize a short article (written) | >= 70% Igbo; <= 5 English fallbacks |
| 4 | Wk 18 | Retell a 3-min story from memory | Write a 150-word message with diacritics | Story events in order; spelling errors <= 5 |
| 5 | Wk 22 | 20-min free conversation, new speaker/dialect | Voice-note exchange in Igbo | No English; can ask for clarification in Igbo |
| Final | Wk 24 | Redo Week 1 baseline | Redo Week 1 baseline | Side-by-side improvement documented |

## "Am I automatic yet?" signals (Phase 4)
- You think a sentence in Igbo before translating it.
- You dream, count, or talk to yourself in Igbo.
- You can switch to Igbo mid-conversation without a "warm-up".
- When you can't remember a word, you describe around it in Igbo instead of switching to English.
