# Checkpoints and Milestones (all spoken)

## Sunday mini-check (15 min, in the 45-min Sunday block)
Score 0-2 each; log in `tracking/PROGRESS.md`.
1. **Say it cold:** 5 sentences using this week's pattern, each in <= 5 seconds, no notes.
2. **Shadow test:** replay Monday's clip; is your recording closer to the speaker than last week?
3. **Recall:** say 10 words/phrases from this week from memory.
4. **Vault spot-check:** 5 random cards of things you already knew; any hesitations?
5. **Streak:** hit at least the 5-minute floor on 6 of 7 days.
Score 8-10 on track; 5-7 add one extra drill; <5 lighten the load and find the blocker.

## "Am I automatic yet?" signals
- You think in Igbo before translating.
- You count, curse, or talk to yourself in Igbo.
- You switch into Igbo mid-conversation with no warm-up.
- When you forget a word, you describe around it in Igbo instead of switching to English.
