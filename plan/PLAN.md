# 24-Week Plan (starts Week 1; ends ~6 months later)

## Pick your daily tier (you can switch weekly)
| Tier | Daily time | Typical week |
|---|---|---|
| **A: Minimum** | 15 min | 5 days x 15 min + 1 x 45 min weekend session |
| **B: Standard (default)** | 30-45 min | 5 days x 30 min + 1 x 60 min tutor/conversation + 1 x 30 min review |
| **C: Intensive** | 60-90 min | Tier B + second tutor session + daily 20 min writing/reading |

Rule: on a bad day, do the **5-minute floor** (one SRS review + say 5 sentences aloud). Never zero. Consistency beats volume.

## The weekly rhythm (Tier B)
| Day | Block (30 min unless noted) |
|---|---|
| Mon | **Grammar focus** (15 min read from GRAMMAR_GAPS + 15 min produce 10 sentences aloud using it) |
| Tue | **Shadowing**: 10 min audio clip, repeat line-by-line, record yourself, compare + 10 min SRS |
| Wed | **Speaking prompt**: talk 5-10 min on a topic (record), then write 5 corrections |
| Thu | **Grammar drill** (same topic as Mon, new sentences) + SRS |
| Fri | **Input**: BBC Igbo / podcast / YouTube, note 5 new words/patterns into SRS |
| Sat | **Tutor or conversation** (45-60 min): bring your Wed recording errors as the agenda |
| Sun | **Review + log**: weekly mini-check (CHECKPOINTS.md), fill PROGRESS.md, plan next week |

## Phases

### Phase 1: Audit and Foundations (Weeks 1-4)
Goal: find your exact gaps, learn to read/write the sounds you already know.
- **Week 1 baseline (do first):** record the 3 baseline tasks in RETENTION.md. Do the gap self-audit in GRAMMAR_GAPS.md.
- Learn orthography: dotted vowels (ị ọ ụ), digraphs (ch gb gh gw kp kw nw ny sh), tone marks. Read aloud daily.
- Tone basics (high / low / downstep). Minimal pairs drill.
- Start the **Gap deck** (new items) and **Vault deck** (things you already know). See RETENTION.md.
- Book your **recurring weekly tutor** now (heritage-learner experience, ideally speaks your dialect region).
- Start `MY_DIALECT_NOTES.md`: ask a parent/relative for 5 phrases a week and ask "how would you say it in a formal setting?".
- **Milestone 1 (end Wk 4):** read a 100-word paragraph aloud with correct sounds; produce 2-min self-introduction + family + daily routine without notes.

### Phase 2: Grammar Formalization (Weeks 5-10)
Goal: put names and rules on patterns you use intuitively, and fix errors you didn't know you made.
- Weeks 5-6: verb tenses/aspects (na-, ga-, -rọ/-rụ, -la/-le, -ghị negation).
- Weeks 7-8: vowel harmony, pronouns/subject prefixes, noun phrases (nke m, ndị, ụmụ), numbers, time.
- Weeks 9-10: questions, relative clauses (nke, onye), conjunctions (na, mana, ma, ọ bụrụ na), commands, polite forms and proverbs/idioms.
- Each week: 10 target sentences you can say with no hesitation, 5 written paragraphs.
- **Milestone 2 (end Wk 10):** 5-min unscripted conversation with tutor, <= 10 flagged grammar errors; correctly explain 3 rules aloud (in English or Igbo).

### Phase 3: Output Production (Weeks 11-18)
Goal: speed and range. Move from "I can say it" to "I say it without thinking."
- Daily 5-min **"Igbo-only inner narration"** (describe what you're doing; note words you lack, look up later).
- Weekly **topic speeches** (work, food, travel, health, opinions, stories, past events, hypotheticals).
- **Retell** a BBC Igbo article or a family story aloud in your own words.
- Start **writing**: a short daily journal entry (3-5 sentences), corrected by tutor weekly.
- Practice the registers: talking to elders (respect forms, greetings), peers, formal settings.
- **Milestone 3 (end Wk 14):** 10-min conversation with tutor/relative on an unplanned topic; speak >= 70% of the time in Igbo with no English fallback more than 5 times.
- **Milestone 4 (end Wk 18):** retell a 3-min audio story from memory; write a 150-word message with correct spelling and tone/dot marks.

### Phase 4: Automaticity and Maintenance (Weeks 19-24)
Goal: make it second nature.
- Switch one real-life routine to Igbo (calls with parents, meals, a group chat, voice notes).
- Longer, harder input: Igbo radio/news, sermons, Nollywood in Igbo without subtitles, proverbs.
- Join a conversation group / language exchange (see RESOURCES.md); aim for 2 conversations per week with non-family speakers.
- Reduce SRS to maintenance; keep recording.
- **Milestone 5 (end Wk 22):** 20-minute free conversation, no English, with someone whose dialect differs from your parents'.
- **Final assessment (Wk 24):** repeat the Week 1 baseline recordings and compare side by side. Choose next 6-month goals.

## Adjustments
- If you miss a week: resume, don't "catch up". Repeat the week's Sunday check only.
- If a milestone fails: repeat that phase's last 2 weeks, with extra tutor time on the failing skill.
- Re-cut the plan any time your schedule changes (work weeks, travel, holidays: use Tier A).
