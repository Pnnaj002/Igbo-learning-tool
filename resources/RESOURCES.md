How to use a tutor: send your weekly recording beforehand; ask them to **interrupt and correct**, not just chat;
ask for the Standard form *and* whether your family's form is OK.

## 6. Standard Igbo vs. your dialect
Standard Igbo (Igbo Izugbe, "Central Igbo") was developed from 1961 on (S.E. Onwu committee) as a blend rather than
a single local variety; sources I found disagree on exactly which dialects it drew from (Owerri/Umuahia area vs. mixed with Onitsha).
Practical meaning: written Igbo and BBC Igbo use it, but nobody's home dialect is exactly it.
A useful reading: [NOUN course on Igbo dialects (PDF)](https://nou.edu.ng/coursewarecontent/IGB%20351%20-%20Igbo%20Dialects.pdf).

## 7. Enugu-specific resources (optional; now secondary)
You chose Standard Igbo as the target, so skip this section unless you want to understand your family's Enugu speech.
Honest note: I found **no Enugu dictionary or Enugu-specific course**. Your best Enugu resource is your Enugu parent, and the Enugu family around them.
- **Enugu-based tutors on Preply/Superprof** (self-described; verify dialect before booking): [Eze S. (Enugu State)](https://preply.com/en/tutor/8107388), [Anikwe D. (Enugu)](https://preply.com/en/tutor/8276614), [Chiamaka N. (grew up in Enugu)](https://preply.com/en/tutor/6356619), [Nneka Rita (Superprof, Enugu, ~$5/hr, free first lesson)](https://www.superprof.com.au/skilled-igbo-language-teacher-personalized-meet-your-needs-all-levels-with-various-teaching-methodology-fine-tune-your.html). I matched these from a search snippet and couldn't confirm which profile page is which; check the names on the listings.
- **Ask any tutor:** "Which part of Enugu are you from? Will you teach me to speak like my parents (Enugu), not just Standard Igbo?" Do a trial lesson with 2-3 and pick the one whose speech matches your parent's.
- **Dialect background:** Enugu State has several Northern Igbo clusters (e.g., the Nsukka group). Research found: [Standard Igbo vs. Igbo North dialects (abstract)](https://www.igbostudies.org/conference/abstracts/a-comparative-study-of-standard-igbo-language-and/), [Opi dialect (Nsukka)](https://nigerianjournalsonline.com/index.php/ODEZURU-IGBO/article/download/4049/3925), [Ika (Nsukka area) phonetics](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-international-phonetic-association/article/ika-igbo/31F6B1E469936708591E4700BA91B8DF). These are academic: they say Northern Igbo differs in sounds (e.g., more vowel elision, extra vowels in some varieties) more than vocabulary. Skim only if curious.
- **Listening:** Enugu relatives' voice notes; Enugu churches/town unions' recordings; Nollywood/radio from Enugu (I found no specific station; ask family). [Speech Accent Archive](https://accent.gmu.edu/language/igbo/) has speakers by origin, so check for ones from Enugu.
- **Other podcasts/YouTube:** [Igbo Daily Drops](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/id1876047354) (Standard Igbo, 10-minute lessons), [The Igbo Tutor / IgboLingua](https://www.udemy.com/user/chukwu-christian-onyemaechi-2/). Useful for vocabulary, but your Enugu parent has the final say on how it sounds.
