# Igbo: From Understanding to Speaking

A personal plan for a heritage speaker: strong listening and conversational comprehension, learned at home
(parents from Enugu and Imo, no formal instruction), with the goal of speaking fluently and automatically.

**Focus: speaking Standard Igbo (Igbo Izugbe), the most widely understood form.** Reading and writing are out of scope for now. BART commute = listening time.

## Core strategy (one paragraph)
You already own the hardest part: a listening-trained ear and intuitive grammar. What's missing is **retrieval under
time pressure** (speaking) and **names for patterns** (formal grammar, reading/writing). So the plan is ~60% output
(speaking, shadowing, writing, tutor), ~25% targeted grammar, ~15% input. Your parents' dialects are an asset: you can keep using them at home, while the plan teaches Standard Igbo, which
Igbo speakers from any region understand (BBC Igbo, schools, church).

## Honest caveats
- Resources were gathered via web search in Nov 2026. Many are commercial; I did not test apps, prices or tutor quality. Check before paying.
- The grammar examples are standard textbook patterns, but your parents' dialect may differ. Verify with a tutor or family member, and log differences in `MY_DIALECT_NOTES.md`.
- The plan assumes a default schedule (see PLAN.md). Tell me your real availability and I'll re-cut it.
